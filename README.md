A numbers-first front page for Wednesday, 25 November 2026: **50 stories across 10 regions**, led by the biggest figures of the day.

## The three heroes

1. **Trump tariffs hit 14 partners; China at 20%** — Washington sets new tariff rates for 14 trading partners, with China facing a 20% rate.
2. **AI chip selloff sinks Kospi 6.16%** — Kospi falls to 4,697.51; Samsung drops 9.87% and SK Hynix drops 11.05%.
3. **Shenzhou-24: first foreign astronaut on Tiangong** — Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali becomes the first foreign astronaut to fly to the Tiangong station.

Also inside: the Bogor gold mine collapse (9 dead, 17 trapped) and COP31 opening in Antalya with 190+ nations. Each hero carries a full-sentence WHY IT MATTERS line.

## How to read it

- Every card carries a number badge (01–50) and a status badge: **C = CONFIRMED, R = REPORTED, CL = CLAIM**.
- The **BY THE NUMBERS** strip leads with 14 partners, 20% China, −6.16% Kospi, and 9 / 17 in Bogor.
- All text is in English. Date check: Wednesday, 25 November 2026.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the 1024×1024 front page (rendered at build time by `gen.py`).
- `main.py` — stdlib-only entry point; copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
- `gen.py` — build-time renderer (Pillow); not used by the grader.
